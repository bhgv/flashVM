# v0.1629 — native big arithmetic and refreshed Dis JITs

Our Coco/R Limbo big-result profile now compiles +, -, *, unary signs, grouping
and arithmetic inside table/key/slice expressions through typed FIR into direct
64-bit Dis ALU instructions. Multiplication binds first; sums/products associate
left. Casts, typeof, len and keysof take unary operands. Exact signed-minimum
literals remain supported. No arithmetic is routed through a native helper RPC.
Compiler version is 0.15.0; matching runtime is 0.18.0.

FIR appends big.add/big.sub/big.mul/big.neg with total modulo-2^64 semantics.
The reference interpreter and peephole use unsigned arithmetic plus portable
signed-bit interpretation, avoiding signed-overflow UB. Optimizations pool exact
big constants, fold known arithmetic and CSE same-block captured SSA expressions.
Effectful reads/conversions survive elimination of unused pure arithmetic.
Source events retain each operator's exact span; negative literals include the
minus and digits. Flat expressions do not exhaust the bounded recursion budget.
