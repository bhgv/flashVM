# v0.1712 — Signed division facts and independent Inferno conformance

FIR implementation0.72 analyzes int32/big quotient and remainder by an exactly
known positive power of two. It derives nonnegative bounds, aligned signed-zero
remainders and common facts across unknown-sign branches. Negative division keeps
truncation toward zero; no arithmetic-shift substitution is made. The private
unsigned-magnitude transfer is a separate commented header. A complete result
folds only with a separate nonzero-divisor proof; eager producer errors and source
origins remain. Public C layouts, Coco/R syntax, FIR1.0, SWO and Dis formats stay.

For example `(x&255)/8` now admits a raw SWF checked shift count; division by4,
negative/zero or unknown unsafe counts still refuse without replacing an existing
object. A fully commented Coco/R Limbo example returns1344 through both targets.
Its detailed walkthrough and proof are linked in English/Russian and installed
with the FIR SDK. The authoritative source grammar now includes string.h for its
existing strcmp-based asinc lookahead; generated sources are not hand-edited.
