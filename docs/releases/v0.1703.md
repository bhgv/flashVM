## Executable task lifetime foundation

`asinctasks.dis` is a new ordinary Limbo library. A sole scheduled owner handles
scalar input/result roots, claim leases, terminal states and owner incarnations
through typed channels. Separate Limbo workers can execute without a timeline
frame. Completion and cancellation have an explicit receive-order winner.
Cancellation of active work retains its input until worker acknowledgement;
results are transferred once. Shutdown refuses unfinished workers.

The service has 32 nonrecycled lifetime IDs, scalar strings up to 65536 characters,
explicit empty/busy/stale/terminal outcomes and detached inspection records.
The complete [EN](../../compiler/runtime/ASINC-TASKS.md)/
[RU](../../compiler/runtime/ASINC-TASKS.ru.md) guides describe ownership,
noncooperative workers, capacity and shutdown policy. A commented `tasks-demo.dis`
example, library/header, acceptance source and bilingual guides ship in the package.

This implements a cooperative task service, not AS closure/FIR lowering, physical
AS threads, mutable captured object graphs or recoverable OOM. Directional capture
publication remains a separate `asincviews` service. Neither service changes
Dis64 wire IDs or enables new native reference-returning calls.
