# v0.1705 — FIR power facts and verified Inferno synchronization

Workspace 0.1705; FIR implementation 0.70.0, textual FIR 1.0 unchanged.

Exact single-bit integer factors now preserve all known bits of their other
operand under modular multiplication. Both orders and the signed MIN factor
work. Other products retain conservative low-zero/parity facts. Unknown bits
are not treated as zero; -2 and 3 are not shifted copies. Producer effects remain.

within 0..28. `(x&7)*8` remains rejected without replacing an existing object.

setter, relocation or fallback. Its current test uses bounded stat/access stand-ins,
not the earlier real-filesystem matrix. Android profile EXTWINFLAGS is explicit
and libm is included. Complete OS/FreeType/NDK/APK ports are not imported or certified.

was independently reproduced against old and current executables. Published the
owning [mk-tempdir regression](https://github.com/bhgv/Inferno-OS-bhgv/blob/experiment/utils/tests/mk-tempdir.py)
as d5429c8. It checks real command-line assignments, spaced temporary paths,
consumed-file cleanup and refusal of missing/overlong directories.

mk executable; standalone DisVM retains its fixture and has no hidden sibling build.

[FLASHVM-0036](https://github.com/bhgv/Inferno-OS-bhgv/blob/experiment/doc/dis64-flashvm-journal.md)
acknowledges the correction and reports the bidirectional regression and FIR work.
No new shared Dis64 ID, reference signature, native builtin or default admission.

remain pending.
