FIR0.64 extends shared successful-result bit facts to big literals/copies,
AND/OR/XOR/complement and checked int32/big shifts with exactly proven legal
int32 counts. Signed right shift propagates a known sign; unknown bits remain
unknown. Compiler arithmetic is unsigned and handles count0/31/63 without
signed overflow or shifting by the host word width.

This admits ordinary AVM1 operations with shift-derived proofs, such as count
`(n & 127) >> 2` or divisor `(d | 1) << 1`. DCE also discards unused big div/mod
when big known bits prove a nonzero divisor. Each producer keeps its own eager
errors; invalid counts and discarded set bits do not prove safety. Mutable cells,
FP/AS coercions, public effects, CSE and ownership remain conservative. No new
parser syntax, opcodes, ABI or big SWF arithmetic is introduced.

CLI, C99 libraries and the actual Dis-owned IDE compiler worker share this path.
The new [fully commented Limbo source](../../fir/v1/examples/dis/shift-facts.b)
returns42 through raw/optimized FIR and established SWF8/NC64 targets. The C99
analysis fixture now checks120832 samples, including unsigned big shift/not
oracles; the new matrix compares272 raw/optimized cross-target results.
