With guarded copies and worker admission, local NEW and NEWZ construct a fresh
record. They capture the Type index under its span guard and validate the
descriptor before allocating. Both initialize mapped slots to H, and NEWZ
also zeros scalar bytes. The candidate has a published root before the monitor.

Conditional publication compares the held destination identity. If a monitor
writes a new destination value, the coordinator refreshes the observation and
retries with the same record. The record contains no dependency on the displaced
value. Cleanup releases every outstanding hold and preserves the first error.
Legacy in-place reuse remains in the unselected path.

The native fixture checks a real interleaving write, one allocation monitor per
record, primary error preservation, initialized fields and eventual Type count
restoration after Cycles and Fast. A Limbo guest checks actual opcode routing in
interpreter and JIT-enabled sessions. Imported MNEWZ, arrays and channels remain
separate constructor work. NativeWorld still protects frame, Type and GC lifetime.

checker on 1536 raw cases. The published storage evidence covers module-wide
