FIR0.65 materializes a verified int32/big bit result as a literal when known-zero
and known-one masks cover its whole width. Dynamic producers can remain: clearing
or forcing a sign then shifting by width-1 proves0/-1; shifting a known odd low
bit to the sign proves MIN. Derived MAX and MIN+123 preserve every big payload bit.
The internal literal helper accepts int64_t for this new full-width call site.

Only admitted bit operations and legal checked shifts qualify. Unknown/invalid
counts keep their checks, and eager producer errors remain observable even when
the outer result becomes constant. No float/dynamic AS identities, memory facts,
reference ownership, effects/CSE policy, grammar, opcode or target ABI changes.
Result identity, source line and block stay attached to the replacement literal.

CLI, C99 and the actual Dis-owned IDE compiler worker use the same optimizer.
The new line-commented own Coco/R source returns42 through raw/optimized Dis and
SWF. SDK acceptance relinks public C99 clients and compares their generated Dis
and SWO bytes with CLI before linking SWF8. Detailed English/Russian guides and
example indexes are connected to the implementation and shipped with libraries.
